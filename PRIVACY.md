# StreamLab Privacy Policy

StreamLab is a personal portfolio project.

- StreamLab does not collect, store, or share any personal data.
- Purchases are processed entirely by Apple. StreamLab never sees your payment details.
- The video catalogue is downloaded from GitHub, which may log standard request information such as your IP address under GitHub's own privacy policy.
- Videos are streamed from public test servers operated by third parties.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Akshayiyerg/StreamLab/issues
